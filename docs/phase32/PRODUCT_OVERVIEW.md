# Product Overview — AA007

## Product
Lead Follow-up Prioritization Intelligence

## Business Problem
Sales teams need a consistent and explainable way to decide which leads require attention.

## Solution
AA007 evaluates lead recency, engagement, declared fit and follow-up commitments through five specialized agents and deterministic business rules.

## Primary Users
- Sales coordinators
- Inside sales representatives
- Sales operations leads

## Business Value
- More consistent follow-up prioritization
- Transparent reasons behind recommendations
- Evidence-based next-action guidance
- Reviewable workflow and audit information

## Decision Authority
Deterministic business rules determine the recommendation. Controlled LLM enhancement supports approved language tasks only. People retain responsibility for customer contact.
