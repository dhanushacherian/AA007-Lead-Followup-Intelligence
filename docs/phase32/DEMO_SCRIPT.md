# AA007 — 2 to 3 Minute Demo Script

## 1. Business Problem (20 seconds)
Sales teams need to prioritize lead follow-up consistently using business evidence instead of manual guesswork.

## 2. Product Input (20 seconds)
Show the lead information and explain the relevant contact, engagement, fit and follow-up fields.

## 3. Agent Workflow (30 seconds)
Explain the five specialized agents and how LangGraph coordinates their execution.

## 4. Recommendation (30 seconds)
Show the deterministic recommendation and explain the business conditions that triggered it.

## 5. Explainability and Audit (25 seconds)
Open the advanced details and show agent outputs, evidence, reasons and audit reference.

## 6. Conditional Scenario (20 seconds)
Demonstrate a different business condition and its corresponding recommendation.

## 7. Controlled LLM (20 seconds)
Show the approved language-enhancement role and, where available, the deterministic fallback. Explain that the LLM cannot change the recommendation.

## 8. Business Value (15 seconds)
AA007 helps sales teams make more consistent, explainable and reviewable follow-up decisions while keeping customer contact under human control.
